I am a first-year PhD student in the Department of Computer Science supervised by Professor Nathan Klein. Previously, I received an MSci from the School of Operations Research and Information Engineering at Cornell University. Before that I obtained my MMath in Combinatorics and Optimization, under the supervision of Joseph Cheriyan from the University of Waterloo, where I also obatined my BMath with an Honours in Combinatorics and Optimization and a Joint Honours in Computer Science.

My research interests are broadly in discrete mathematics and discrete optimization. Currently, I am mainly interested in approximation algorithms for Network Design problems.
